01 / About

My best work lives at the intersection.

I'm interested in the point where place, data and people meet.

My background in computational and applied mathematics, economics and GIS lets me approach a problem from more than one angle. I can examine the evidence, map the system, understand the user journey and help shape a solution that makes sense in the real world.

Whether I'm building an ArcGIS workflow, analysing time-series data or teaching a difficult concept, the goal is the same: make complexity easier to understand—and easier to act on.
